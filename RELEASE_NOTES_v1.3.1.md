# JuicyChat Creator Tool v1.3.1

## Changes
- **PNG Compressor is now Image Compressor.** Hover the tab to see what it does: *Compress your images to under 1MB.*
- **The Introduction Editor is the same everywhere.** Opening it from its tab now shows **Save and Return to Bot Builder** too, just like opening it with **Build Introduction** in the Bot Builder. From the tab, it asks first, then saves the Introduction into the bot that's open in the Bot Builder. If no bot is open yet, it tells you to choose one first.

## Unchanged
- Everything else works as in v1.3.0. Saved bots, designs and templates keep working.
- Everything stays on your computer. No uploads.

## Compatibility
- Windows 64-bit and Linux 64-bit

## Updating
**Check for updates → Update & Restart** installs this version.
